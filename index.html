Author & Speaker Bio

Maham Khalid is an author, speaker, and educator working at the intersection of human behavior, leadership, and the future being reshaped by artificial intelligence. Her work helps individuals and organizations navigate accelerating change, shifting skill demands, and the expanding role of AI in how we live and work. Her gift is translation — taking what feels overwhelming and making it usable. Whether in a classroom, boardroom, or on stage, she builds spaces where comfortable assumptions don’t survive long, and people are challenged to think more honestly about the lives they’re building.
