Author & Speaker Bio

Maham Khalid is an author, speaker, and educator working at the intersection of human behavior, leadership, and the future being rewritten by artificial intelligence. An award-winning voice in leadership, innovation, and community impact, she helps individuals and organizations navigate accelerating change, shifting skill demands, and the expanding role of AI in how we live and work.

Her gift is translation — taking what feels overwhelming and making it usable, closing the gap between what people learn and what they actually do with it. Whether in a classroom, boardroom, or on stage, she builds the same kind of space: one where comfortable assumptions don't survive long, and people are challenged to think more honestly about the lives they're building.
